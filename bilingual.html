Hacker News post claims $5.6B stolen from Binance

A Hacker News post claims $5.6 billion was stolen from Binance, with roughly $2.4 billion reportedly frozen by stablecoin issuers Circle and Tether. The claim is unverified and has no primary confirmation from Binance or the issuers.

PHP2Shell 与 Linux XFRM 漏洞串联利用，据报攻陷 25 台服务器

Dream Group 的一篇博客报告称，攻击者将 PHP2Shell 漏洞与 Linux 内核 XFRM ESP-in-TCP 子系统中的漏洞串联利用，攻陷了 25 台服务器，其中包括美国政府系统。该报告将这一链条描述为两阶段攻击：先通过 PHP2Shell 获得初始立足点，再借助内核漏洞提升权限。上述内容来自单一事件报告，目前没有独立验证或厂商确认，具体受影响版本、补丁状态和披露时间线均未在现有材料中说明。

Chained PHP2Shell and Linux XFRM Exploit Reportedly Roots 25 Servers

A blog post from Dream Group reports that a chained exploit combining a PHP2Shell flaw with a Linux kernel vulnerability in the XFRM ESP-in-TCP subsystem rooted 25 servers, including US government systems. The report describes the chain as using PHP2Shell for initial access and the XFRM kernel bug for privilege escalation. No source content was supplied, so the specific CVE identifiers, affected versions, patch status, and independent verification of the 25-server count remain unconfirmed. The claim rests on a single incident report rather than a vendor advisory or measured third-party analysis.

European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde is reportedly set to resign early, according to The Wall Street Journal, and Europe&\#x27;s largest countries are already competing over who will succeed her. 七艘运载俄罗斯柴油的油轮驶向美国

据绿色和平组织追踪，七艘油轮正载着约 28.8 万吨俄罗斯柴油驶向美国，这是特朗普与普京本月早些时候达成的供应协议的第一阶段，该协议逆转了多年来对俄罗斯石油的制裁；这些船只能否卸货仍不确定，因为围绕这批货物合法性存在政治争议。

Russian diesel tankers head to US under Trump-Putin deal

Seven tankers carrying an estimated 288,000 tonnes of Russian diesel are sailing toward the US as the first phase of a supply deal between Presidents Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin, according to tracking by Greenpeace. It is unclear whether the vessels will be able to unload, amid a political dispute over the shipments&\#x27; legality.

Alphabet 第三季度利润下滑 25%，市值蒸发约 5000 亿美元

谷歌母公司 Alphabet 公布第三季度净利润同比下降 25%至 262 亿美元，低于华尔街预期，营收则升至 1276 亿美元；财报发布后股价连续两日大跌两位数，市值蒸发约 5000 亿美元。

Alphabet Shares Plunge After Profit Miss

Alphabet shares fell by double digits after the Google parent reported third-quarter net profit down 25% from a year earlier to $26.2bn, missing Wall Street expectations, while revenue rose to $127.6bn. The decline erased about $500bn in market value from the roughly $4tn company, according to Agence France-Presse.

英特尔股价暴跌 20%，AI 热潮波及 PC 芯片业务

英特尔股价下跌 20%，此前这家芯片制造商报告称，AI 热潮的连锁反应拖累了其对个人电脑制造商的销售，并预计 PC 芯片销售低于预期、利润率下滑。

Intel Shares Fall 20% as AI Boom Weighs on PC Chip Sales

Intel shares fell 20% after the chipmaker reported that the AI boom&\#x27;s knock-on effects hurt sales to personal computer makers, and it forecast lower-than-expected PC chip sales and falling margins. The company&\#x27;s largest investor is the U.S. government.

美日同意在日本设厂组装空对空导弹

美国和日本同意在日本建立一座工厂，用于组装关键的空对空导弹。报道称，此举背景是美方导弹库存减少，以及对中国的担忧上升；报道未提供投资金额、产能或时间表。

U.S. and Japan Agree to Assemble Air-to-Air Missiles in Japan

The U.S. and Japan agreed to set up a facility in Japan to assemble key air-to-air missiles, as U.S. stockpiles run low and concerns about China grow. The report gives no figures, timeline, or contract value.
